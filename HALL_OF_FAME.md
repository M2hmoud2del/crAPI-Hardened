# Hall of Fame

Welcome to the crAPI-Hardened Hall of Fame! This is our way of publicly thanking the security researchers and contributors who have responsibly disclosed vulnerabilities and helped improve the security of this project.

## Recognition Categories

### 🩸 First Blood
Researchers who are the absolute first to discover and responsibly disclose a critical or high-severity vulnerability in the project. These are the pioneers who set the bar for security.

### 🏆 Elite Researchers
Researchers who discover valid vulnerabilities in the latest stable release.

### 🥈 Security Contributors
Researchers who discover valid vulnerabilities in older releases or identify lower-severity issues.

### 🥉 Community Contributors
People who contribute fixes, reviews, testing, documentation, discussions, or other valuable contributions.
